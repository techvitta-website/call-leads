The honest part

Every one of these came back marked "Unverified", and that's the model telling the truth.

Look at the phone numbers: 9876543210, …211, …212, …213. Sequential. They're placeholders, not real numbers. The email addresses follow a guessed firstname.lastname@domain pattern, and the contact names are plausible rather than known.

So what is this actually good for? The targeting and the reasoning are the valuable output here — which sub-verticals fit an HRMS pitch, why a pharma plant feels this pain differently from a textile unit, and what trigger to open the call with. That's genuinely useful prep.

The contact details are a starting point for research, not a dialling list. Treat a generated lead as "here is a company worth looking up", then verify the person and number before anyone calls. The confidence badge exists so a rep can tell at a glance which is which, rather than discovering it on a dead line.
