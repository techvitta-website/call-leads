The unexpected part: the companies got better too.

Run 1 produced generic-sounding names — Zenith, Delta, Futura, PolyPack — that don't correspond to anything in particular. Run 2 returned Virchow Biotech, Sri Chakra Cements, Deccan Auto: actual Hyderabad and Vijayawada manufacturers, with real domains.

The pattern seems to be that when the model had to produce a plausible person for every row, inventing the company was the path of least resistance. Remove that pressure and it reaches for firms it genuinely knows. Confidence went from low on all six to high on all five — and that self-report now matches reality.

Batch filterThe subtle case: numbers that each look fine but share the first 8 digits across the batch — i.e. a sequence. All six of Run 1's numbers are stripped by this.
