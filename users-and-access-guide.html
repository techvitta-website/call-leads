Users & Access

Managing logins, roles and passwords on sales.techvitta.in

You can now add people, change what they're allowed to see, reset a forgotten password and switch a login off — all from inside the CRM, without opening the Supabase dashboard. Sign in as venkatesh@techvitta.in and open Users & Access in the sidebar.

What each role can do

RoleSeesCan manage people?
Owner Everything. Everything, on anyone — roles included.
Manager All leads, projects, automations and the AI tools. Add salespeople, reset their passwords, switch their logins off. Cannot change anyone's role, and cannot touch another manager or an owner.
Salesperson Only the leads assigned to them. Nothing. The page isn't reachable for them at all.

Role assignment being owner-only is deliberate. If a manager could grant the manager role, any one of them could quietly mint peers and the owner tier would stop meaning anything.

The things you'll actually do

Reset someone's password

Find them in the list, press Reset password. A strong password is suggested — press the copy button, save it, then press Set password. It takes effect immediately and signs them out everywhere else. Send it to them over something private and ask them to change it.

There's no "email them a reset link" flow here; this is a deliberate hand-off so you always know the account is back in the right hands.

Stop someone signing in

Switch off Can sign in. This is the right move when someone leaves — their leads, calls and history all stay exactly where they are, and you can switch it straight back on. Deleting is permanent and asks you to hand their leads to someone else first.

Take someone out of the lead rotation

Each salesperson has a Gets new leads switch. New leads from the website, Instagram, LinkedIn and the AI tools are handed to whoever has the fewest open leads — which means a dormant account sitting at zero absorbs everything. Switch it off for anyone who shouldn't be receiving work. Leads they already hold aren't affected.

Add a person

Add person, fill in name and email, pick the role, copy the suggested password. They can sign in straight away — no confirmation email.

Guards that will stop you

These refuse rather than let you break something:

Demoting yourselfLocked in the UI. It would take away this page and need another owner to undo.
Suspending or deleting yourselfRefused.
Demoting the last owner who can sign inRefused. Promote someone else to owner first.
Deleting someone who holds leadsRefused until you pick who inherits them.
A manager changing any roleRefused.
A manager touching another manager or an ownerRefused.

Every one of these is enforced on the server, not just hidden in the interface, and your role is re-read from the database on every single request — so a browser session opened before a demotion carries no leftover authority.
